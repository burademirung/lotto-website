Frequently Asked Questions

How does LottoLab generate numbers?

LottoLab uses five different methods to generate lottery numbers: GPS location, celestial data (planets, moon, stars), weather conditions, market data (crypto, stocks), and mathematical patterns. Each method uses real-world data to create unique number combinations.

Can LottoLab predict winning numbers?

No. LottoLab is for entertainment purposes only. Lottery drawings are completely random events, and no app, system, or strategy can predict winning numbers. The app uses interesting data sources to generate numbers, but these have no predictive value.

Why does the app need location access?

Location is only used when you choose GPS Mode to generate numbers. Your coordinates are processed entirely on your device and never sent to our servers. You can deny permission and still use all other generation modes.

How do I save my numbers?

Go to the "My Numbers" tab, select the lottery type (Powerball or Mega Millions), and tap "+ Add" to enter your numbers. You can save up to 5 sets per lottery.

How does match checking work?

When you open the app, it automatically checks your saved numbers against recent lottery drawings. If any of your numbers match, you'll see a notification with details about the match.

What are hot, cold, and overdue numbers?

Hot numbers appear more frequently than average in recent draws. Cold numbers appear less frequently. Overdue numbers haven't appeared in a longer time than expected. Remember: past results don't influence future drawings.

Is my data private?

Yes. All your saved numbers and preferences are stored locally on your device. We don't collect personal information or track your usage. See our Privacy Policy for details.

The historical data shows "Simulated" - what does that mean?

When the app can't fetch real lottery data (due to network issues), it uses simulated data to demonstrate the analysis features. Real data is fetched from official sources when available.
