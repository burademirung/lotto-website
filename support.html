Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the lottery data come from?

All drawing results are fetched from the official New York State Open Data portal (data.ny.gov), a government-operated public data source. Results are updated automatically after each drawing.

How do the AI predictions work?

Lottery Pro uses four prediction strategies — neural network, frequency-based ML, pattern recognition, and an ensemble model that combines all three. Each prediction includes a confidence score. These are statistical models based on historical patterns. No prediction system can guarantee lottery wins.

What do I get with Pro?

Pro unlocks unlimited number generations, all 11 pattern analyses, AI/ML predictions, the visual heatmap generator, pool/syndicate manager, ticket tracking, and an ad-free experience. Available at $9.99/month or $79.99/year (save 33%).

How do I restore my subscription?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID and will restore automatically on any device signed into the same account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > Lottery Pro > Cancel. You will retain access until the end of your billing period.

Is my data private?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect personal information, require accounts, or transmit data to any server. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

The app is not loading drawing data.

Ensure you have an active internet connection. The app downloads historical data on first launch and fetches updates automatically. Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, contact us.
