Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 1, 2026

Lottery Pro ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. Lottery Pro operates entirely on your device. We do not require account creation, login, or any personal information to use the app.

Data stored locally on your device includes:

  • Lottery drawing history (fetched from public government APIs)
  • Saved ticket entries you create
  • Achievement progress and app preferences
  • Subscription status (managed by Apple)

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking purposes
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Financial information (all payments handled by Apple)

3. Third-Party Services

NY Open Data API: The app fetches official lottery drawing results from the New York State Open Data portal (data.ny.gov). This is a public government API. No personal data is sent with these requests.

Google AdMob: Free users may see ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers do not see ads.

Apple StoreKit: Subscription purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

4. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally in a SQLite database on your device. No data is transmitted to our servers because we do not operate any servers. If you delete the app, all local data is permanently removed.

5. Children's Privacy

Lottery Pro is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control. You can delete all data at any time by deleting the app. No data deletion request to us is necessary.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
