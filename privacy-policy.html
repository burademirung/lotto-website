Privacy Policy

LottoLab - Lottery Number Generator

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to LottoLab ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Quick Summary: LottoLab is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data, do not sell your information, and most features work entirely on your device.

Information We Collect

1. Location Data (Optional)

When you use GPS Mode, we request access to your device's location. This data is:

  • Used only to generate lottery numbers
  • Processed entirely on your device
  • Never transmitted to our servers
  • Never stored permanently

2. Locally Stored Data

The following data is stored only on your device:

  • Your saved lottery numbers (My Numbers feature)
  • App preferences and settings
  • Disclaimer acceptance status

3. Network Requests

The app makes network requests to third-party APIs for:

  • Historical lottery results (data.ny.gov)
  • Weather data (Open-Meteo API)
  • Market data (public APIs)
  • ISS position data (NASA API)

These requests may include your approximate location (for weather) but do not include any personally identifiable information.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone)
  • Financial information
  • Device identifiers for tracking
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Advertising identifiers

Data Storage and Security

All user data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. We do not operate servers that store user data.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • data.ny.gov - For historical lottery results
  • Open-Meteo - For weather data
  • Public APIs - For market and celestial data

Please refer to these services' privacy policies for information about how they handle data.

Children's Privacy

LottoLab is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older (or the legal gambling age in their jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app
  • Deny location permissions
  • Use the app offline (with limited features)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
